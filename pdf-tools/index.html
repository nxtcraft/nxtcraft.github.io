Why Use NxtCraft PDF Tools Instead of iLovePDF or SmallPDF?

Most online PDF tools upload your files to their servers — including sensitive documents like salary slips, Aadhaar copies, bank statements, and legal agreements. NxtCraft tools process everything entirely in your browser using JavaScript — no file ever leaves your device.

These tools work without internet after first load (Progressive Web Apps), so you can use them on flights or in areas with poor connectivity. No subscription, no watermark, no file size limit imposed by a paywall.

For businesses: InvoiceLab generates GST-compliant invoices with GSTIN, HSN codes, and place of supply — ready to share with clients as PDF. The Salary Slip Generator creates professional payslips with all statutory deductions for HR teams.
