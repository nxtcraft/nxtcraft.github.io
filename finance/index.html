Free Financial Calculators for India — No Signup, No Ads Popups

These tools are built for Indian taxpayers, salaried employees, freelancers, and investors. All calculations follow Indian tax laws, SEBI regulations, and RBI guidelines. Every tool works entirely in your browser — no data is sent to any server.

Tax season (Jan–Mar): Use the Old vs New Tax Regime Calculator to decide before submitting your investment declarations to HR. The 87A rebate under New Regime makes income up to ₹12 lakh tax-free in FY 2025-26.

Changing jobs? Use the FnF Calculator to know your expected payout before the last working day. The Gratuity Calc helps check if your employer owes you gratuity (requires 5 years continuous service under Payment of Gratuity Act).

Planning investments? Compare SIP vs lumpsum in the SIP Calculator, or check your EPF/PPF maturity in the EPF·PPF·SSY tool.
