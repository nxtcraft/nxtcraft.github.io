Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

NxtCraft ("we", "us") operates the website at nxtcraft.github.io and all its sub-tools (PDFCraft, WordKit, EMICalc, QRCraft, InvoiceKit, ResumeKit, ShopBook, TradeCalc, DevToolbox, TaxCalc, QuickTools, PromptKit). This page explains our data practices.

1. No Personal Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All calculations, document generation, and tool outputs happen entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server.

2. Local Storage

Some tools (ShopBook, ResumeKit, PromptKit) use your browser's localStorage to save your data between sessions. This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us or any third party. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Cookies

NxtCraft itself does not set any cookies. However, we may display Google AdSense advertisements, which may use cookies to show relevant ads. Google's use of advertising cookies is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings.

4. Third-Party Libraries

Some tools load JavaScript libraries from CDNs (e.g., jsPDF from Cloudflare CDN, QR code libraries). These CDNs may log standard server access data (IP address, browser type, timestamp) in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not receive or have access to this data.

5. Analytics

We may use Google Analytics or Google Search Console to understand aggregate usage (page views, search queries). This data is anonymised and aggregated — we cannot identify individual users. No personally identifiable information is collected.

6. Advertising

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. These ads may be personalised based on your browsing history as managed by Google. To opt out of personalised ads, visit Google Ads Settings or install the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

7. Children's Privacy

NxtCraft tools are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the tools after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our About page.
