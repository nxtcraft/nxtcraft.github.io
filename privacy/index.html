Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026 · Last updated: November 5, 2026

Short version: NxtCraft does not collect, store, or sell your personal data. All tool computations (PDF processing, image editing, audio transcription) happen entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter into any tool is ever sent to our servers — because we don't have any.

1. Who We Are

NxtCraft ("we", "us", "our") operates the website https://nxtcraft.github.io — a free collection of browser-based tools for finance, PDF, image, documents, AI, and developer utilities.

Contact: nxtcraft.tools@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

We collect no personal information directly. We do not have user accounts, sign-up forms, or any mechanism to identify you as an individual.

We use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to understand aggregate usage patterns. GA4 collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent
  • Device type (mobile, desktop, tablet)
  • Browser and operating system
  • Approximate country/region (city-level precision is not stored)
  • Referring website (how you arrived at NxtCraft)

This data is anonymised and aggregated. We cannot use it to identify any individual visitor. Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC under their own privacy policy.

3. How Your Data Stays on Your Device

Every tool on NxtCraft processes your input locally in your browser:

  • PDF tools (compress, merge, split, password, text extract, Aadhaar masking) — your PDF files never leave your device
  • Image tools (background remover, compressor, converter, QR code) — images are processed in-browser using WebAssembly and Canvas API
  • AI Transcription — the Whisper speech recognition model is downloaded to your browser and runs locally; your audio is never uploaded to any server
  • AI Grammar — text is sent to Claude AI (Anthropic) via a client-side API call for grammar correction. No text is stored by NxtCraft.
  • Financial calculators — all EMI, SIP, tax, GST, and other calculations are performed in JavaScript in your browser

No tool on NxtCraft requires a file upload to our servers, because we operate no servers.

4. Cookies

We use the following cookies set by Google Analytics:

  • _ga — distinguishes unique users (expires 2 years)
  • _ga_R9EW10BJGW — session-level data for GA4 (expires 2 years)

These are first-party analytics cookies only. We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels, or social media cookies.

Future advertising: We may add Google AdSense to fund this free service. If we do, advertising cookies (_gads, _gcl_au, etc.) will be added and this policy will be updated with a prominent notice at least 7 days in advance.

You can opt out of Google Analytics at any time by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on or enabling "Do Not Track" in your browser settings.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymous usage statistics. Google Privacy Policy ↗
  • GitHub Pages — website hosting. GitHub Privacy Statement ↗
  • Anthropic Claude API — used only for the AI Grammar tool (text submitted for grammar correction is not stored by NxtCraft). Anthropic Privacy Policy ↗

6. Children's Privacy

NxtCraft is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child has used our site and provided information through a third-party service we integrate, please contact us at nxtcraft.tools@gmail.com.

7. Your Rights

Since NxtCraft collects no personal data directly, most data subject rights are exercised with Google for Analytics data. You have the right to:

  • Opt out of Google Analytics (see Section 4)
  • Request any information we may hold about you (contact us — in practice we hold none)
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

GDPR (EU/EEA users): You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection under the General Data Protection Regulation.

DPDPA (Indian users): This policy is consistent with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. No personal data is collected or processed by NxtCraft directly.

8. Data Security

Since all processing happens in your browser and NxtCraft maintains no servers or databases, there is no server-side personal data at risk. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages with HTTPS enforced on all pages.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, when we add Google AdSense. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. We encourage you to check this page periodically.

10. Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, email us at nxtcraft.tools@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 7 working days.
